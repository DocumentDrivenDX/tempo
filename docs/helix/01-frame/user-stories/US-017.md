# US-017: Close an accounting period

**Feature**: FEAT-005 — Entry Approval
**Feature Requirements**: see FEAT-005
**PRD Requirements**: FR-27
**Priority**: P0

## Story

**As a** Dana, Accountant
**I want** to close the books through a date, even if some entries are unapproved
**So that** hours used for billing and payroll cannot change afterwards

## Context

Accounting owns period close; approval is per entry and does not by itself freeze a period.

## Walkthrough

1. Accountant opens period close
2. Accountant chooses a close-through date
3. System shows pending entries on or before that date
4. Accountant confirms
5. System locks all entries on or before the date and records the action

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **US-017-AC1** — Given a close-through date of 31 May, when an Accountant confirms the close, then no entry with a local date on or before 31 May can be created, edited, deleted, approved, rejected, or reopened
- [ ] **US-017-AC2** — Given entries on or before the date that are still unapproved, when the Accountant closes the period, then the close succeeds and those entries are locked and stay unapproved
- [ ] **US-017-AC3** — Given a closed period, when a Manager or Member tries to change an entry in it, then the change is denied
- [ ] **US-017-AC4** — Given a closed period, when an Admin tries to change an entry in it directly, then the change is denied until the period is reopened
- [ ] **US-017-AC5** — Given a closed period, when an Accountant reopens it by moving the date earlier, then entries after the new date become changeable and the audit trail records actor, time, and reason
- [ ] **US-017-AC6** — Given a Member or Manager, when they try to close or reopen a period, then the action is denied
- [ ] **US-017-AC7** — Given an Admin, when they close or reopen a period, then the action succeeds and the audit trail records the Admin as actor

## Edge Cases

- Pending entries inside the period at close: locked unapproved; reports can filter by approval status.
- Entry local date, not UTC, decides which period it belongs to.
